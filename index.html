As a writer, I focus on existentialist philosophy and my main publication is called Servant of Darkness, Master of Light.

One of the main points I make in this work is that happiness or fulfillment in life depends on a meticulous balance of pleasure and penance.

Only by constantly challenging mind and body, can their power be cultivated and thus our sufferings in life diminished and overcome.

An infinite journey about which I constantly learn more too, which in turn results in more updates for the book.

You can currently find it in the three languages I speak comfortably, but more versions are planned for the future.
